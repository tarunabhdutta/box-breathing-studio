4-7-8 breathing: the relaxing breath for sleep

The 4-7-8 breathing technique, popularised by Dr Andrew Weil, uses a short inhale, a longer hold and a long, slow exhale. People use it to wind down at bedtime and to reset after a stressful moment. The long exhale is the key part.

How to do 4-7-8 breathing, step by step

  1. Sit or lie comfortably. Rest the tip of your tongue behind your upper front teeth.
  2. Exhale fully through your mouth.
  3. Inhale quietly through your nose for 4.
  4. Hold your breath for 7.
  5. Exhale through your mouth for 8, with a soft whoosh.
  6. Beginners often start with 4 rounds and build up slowly.

4-7-8 breathing in Box Breathing Studio

The 4-7-8 routine runs 8 guided rounds with a voice that tells you when to breathe in, hold and breathe out. If 7 seconds is too long, keep the same ratio at a faster count, or use the Extended Exhale (4 in, 6 out) routine instead.

Why people practise 4-7-8 breathing

  • The exhale is twice as long as the inhale, which many people find deeply calming.
  • Counting gives a busy mind something to hold on to at bedtime.
  • It takes about two minutes and needs nothing but your breath.

Who should be careful

  • High blood pressure or heart conditions: skip the breath holds. Use Extended Exhale, or the Custom Builder with the hold set to 0.
  • Pregnancy: avoid holding the breath.
  • Dizziness or light-headedness: stop and breathe normally. Shorten the counts next time.
  • Breathing exercises support well-being. They are not a treatment for any medical or mental-health condition. Speak to a doctor if you have a health concern.

Frequently asked questions

Does 4-7-8 breathing help you sleep?

Many people use it as part of a bedtime routine to relax. Results vary from person to person.

How many rounds of 4-7-8 should I do?

Start with 4 rounds. The app's guided routine runs 8 rounds; stop sooner if you feel light-headed.

What if I can't hold my breath for 7 seconds?

Keep the 4:7:8 ratio but count faster, or use a routine without holds such as Extended Exhale.
