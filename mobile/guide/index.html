Free guided breathing exercises app

Box Breathing Studio is a free, open-source breathing and meditation app with calm voice guidance. It works fully offline, has no ads and needs no account. Use it on Android, iPhone or Windows to practise breathing exercises for sleep, stress, focus and long days at a desk.

Who should be careful

  • High blood pressure or heart conditions: skip the breath holds. Use Extended Exhale, or the Custom Builder with the hold set to 0.
  • Pregnancy: avoid holding the breath.
  • Dizziness or light-headedness: stop and breathe normally. Shorten the counts next time.
  • Breathing exercises support well-being. They are not a treatment for any medical or mental-health condition. Speak to a doctor if you have a health concern.

Frequently asked questions

Is Box Breathing Studio really free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT licence, with no ads, no signup, no subscription and no data collection.

Does the breathing app work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the whole app, including voice and images (about 16 MB), is stored on your device.

Which breathing exercise is best for sleep?

Many people use 4-7-8 breathing or Extended Exhale at bedtime. The app's Smart Wizard asks three quick questions and suggests one.

Which breathing exercise is good for anxiety and stress?

Box breathing, Bhramari and Extended Exhale are popular for calming down. They are self-help techniques, not a substitute for professional care.

Get Box Breathing Studio free

Phone (Android or iPhone): open the app, then choose Install app or Add to Home Screen. It works offline after the first visit.

Windows 10/11: download the desktop app, unzip it and run install_app.bat.
